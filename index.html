The routes are the doorway.
The Spanish property graph is the product.

Spain is not a flat parcel file. A useful property layer has to preserve cadastral reference identity, municipality context, addresses, parcel geometry, building relationships, source revisions and coverage states without pretending protected or unavailable facts exist.

01

04

Building relationships

Connect buildings and building parts to cadastral property identity where the official graph supports it.

05

Coordinate resolution

Turn latitude / longitude into property context only where promoted geometry supports a deterministic answer.

06

Municipality routing

Normalize thousands of municipality-scoped source packages behind one country contract.

07

Provenance

Keep source family, municipality, source revision and evidence lineage with property facts.

08

Protected-data boundary

Ownership and cadastral value stay outside the open-data contract unless a lawful, separately entitled source exists.

09

A property match, geometry match and dataset-coverage state are separate signals — because they mean different things.

Spain’s source graph contains real-world drift: repeated address identities, changing points, building-to-parcel anomalies and municipality-specific revisions. PropData certifies those conditions before promotion instead of hiding them behind a happy-path API.

01

Exact parcel required

02

Protected fields excluded

No ownership or cadastral value is requested by the open-data launch path.

03

Drift is evidence

Source geometry and address drift is classified, versioned and governed rather than overwritten.

04

Promotion is earned

Municipality-scale canaries, reconciliation and zero-regression gates precede production promotion.
