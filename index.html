LAUNCH ENGINEERING

“Close enough” is not a property match.

Spain’s source graph contains real-world drift: repeated address identities, changing points, building-to-parcel anomalies and municipality-specific revisions. PropData certifies those conditions before promotion instead of hiding them behind a happy-path API.

01

Exact parcel required

Property detail is attached only to an existing canonical parcel identity.

02

Protected fields excluded

No ownership or cadastral value is requested by the open-data launch path.

03

Drift is evidence

Source geometry and address drift is classified, versioned and governed rather than overwritten.

04

Promotion is earned

Municipality-scale canaries, reconciliation and zero-regression gates precede production promotion.
